Financial Literacy

Empowering communities to manage resources, save, and invest for a better future.

Why Financial Literacy Matters

Many communities in Nairobi and across Kenya face challenges managing their personal and household finances. Limited knowledge of budgeting, saving, and investing often leads to cycles of debt and missed opportunities for growth.

In a world increasingly driven by digital finance, many are unaware of the tools and platforms that can help manage money effectively.

Our Approach

Narudi Initiative organizes workshops and training sessions that focus on practical skills for financial management. We partner with professionals from banks and financial institutions to provide hands-on guidance on digital banking, savings, investments, and entrepreneurship.

Our programs help communities understand how to plan, budget, and grow resources responsibly, fostering economic independence and stability.

Programs and Activities

  • Workshops on budgeting, saving, and investment strategies
  • Hands-on sessions with banking professionals on digital banking and secure transactions
  • Guidance on entrepreneurship and small business management
  • Training on financial goal setting and long-term planning
  • Community mentoring programs to track progress and encourage responsible financial behavior

The Impact

Financial literacy empowers individuals to make informed decisions, avoid debt traps, and take advantage of investment opportunities. Communities become more resilient, households thrive, and youth are better prepared to create sustainable livelihoods.

With proper guidance and access to digital tools, even low-income families can plan for emergencies, education, and future growth.
