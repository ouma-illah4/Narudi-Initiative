Supporting emotional well-being and resilience in our communities.

The Mental Health Crisis in Nairobi

Mental health challenges are rising across Kenya, especially in urban centers like Nairobi. Many individuals who appear healthy and happy are secretly struggling with anxiety, depression, and emotional stress. Studies indicate that in every 5 people, 2 may be experiencing depression or significant mental health challenges.

Rapid technological growth and urbanization have disrupted traditional community structures, which historically provided stability. Modernization, while offering freedom and opportunities, has also introduced loneliness, stress, and anxiety, with broken family units and strained relationships exacerbating the problem.

Key Facts

1 in 5 Kenyans experience mental health challenges
Depression and anxiety are rising among youth and urban residents
Mental health stigma prevents many from seeking help
Suicide rates are increasing, especially among young adults

Our Approach at Narudi Initiative

We aim to break the stigma and provide support through education, counseling, and community engagement. Our programs include talks, seminars, and workshops on mental health awareness for schools, youth groups, and communities.

We emphasize early recognition of mental health challenges and connect individuals to appropriate support systems, including counseling and therapy where needed.

Programs and Activities

  • Mental health seminars and awareness talks in schools and communities
  • Workshops on coping strategies for stress, anxiety, and depression
  • Peer mentorship and support groups
  • Referral to professional counseling or therapy when required
  • Community campaigns to reduce stigma and encourage help-seeking

The Impact

Through awareness, education, and professional guidance, we help individuals recognize challenges early, build coping skills, and seek support before issues escalate. Communities become more compassionate, and young people gain tools to navigate modern pressures healthily.
