About Narudi Initiative

NARUDI INITIATIVE COMMUNITY BASED ORGANISATION (CBO) is a Christian-based organization located in Makadara Sub-County, Nairobi. We voluntarily work to bring positive change to the community, focusing on health, education, and sustainable development.

Contact us: P.O. BOX 17056-00510, Nairobi | Tel: 0719530016 | Email: narudiinitiative@gmail.com

Our Mission

To become a beacon of hope bringing transformation to the community.

Our Vision

To be vessels that bring light to the life of individuals in the community, whose souls are shadowed by social injustice, substance abuse, and broken families. We give each a chance to grow and become destiny changers to others.

Our Objectives

  • Defend human rights and raise awareness through civic education.
  • Enhance public participation in decision-making and resource management.
  • Uplift livelihoods through financial literacy programs.
  • Tackle economic disparities and poverty to address root causes of unrest.
  • Build resilience and adaptation to the effects of climate change.
  • Raise mental health awareness in schools and communities.

Membership

Members must be 18 years or older, of sound mind, and willing to abide by the CBO rules and regulations. A non-refundable registration fee is required. Members have rights, privileges, and obligations as outlined in the constitution.

Funding & Use of Funds

Our CBO is funded through registration fees, donations, fundraisers, investments, and contributions from well-wishers. All funds are used for programs and initiatives approved by the Executive Committee and members.
