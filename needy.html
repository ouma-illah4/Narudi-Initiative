The Reality in Kenya

Despite economic growth, many households in Kenya continue to face significant challenges in meeting basic needs. According to the World Bank, a large portion of the population still lives on low incomes, making it difficult to consistently afford food, shelter, and essential services.

Rising living costs, especially in urban areas like Nairobi, have placed additional pressure on families. For many, income does not match the cost of survival.

Key Challenges

About 36% of Kenyans Live Below the Poverty Line
Youth Unemployment Remains High
Over 60% of Nairobi Residents Live in Informal Settlements
Rising Cost of Food and Housing

These conditions create a cycle where families struggle daily to secure even the most basic necessities.

The Human Impact

The effects of poverty are visible across communities. Many children are forced onto the streets, and homelessness continues to rise as families are unable to sustain housing.

Without intervention, vulnerable individuals face hunger, exposure, and limited opportunities for a better future.

What We Do

Through the support of generous donors like you, Narudi Initiative works to meet immediate needs while restoring dignity and hope.

  • Provide food support to vulnerable families
  • Distribute clothing and essential supplies
  • Assist individuals facing homelessness
  • Support children living on the streets

While we strive to reach as many people as possible, the number of individuals in need continues to grow. The demand for support often exceeds available resources.
