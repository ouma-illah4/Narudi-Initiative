Civic Education

Empowering communities to know their rights and participate actively in society.

The Situation in Nairobi

Unfortunately, in Nairobi, the rise of corruption and inequality is often fueled by ignorance. Many citizens do not know their rights and privileges, particularly in matters of leadership and civic participation. Awareness of what the government has in place is limited, which reduces accountability and citizen involvement.

Civic ignorance can result in marginalized voices, unchallenged corruption, and a cycle where communities are not fully empowered to shape their own future.

Our Approach

Through civic education programs, we create awareness about citizens’ rights, duties, and governance structures. We equip communities with the knowledge needed to actively participate in public life and hold leaders accountable.

Our programs include workshops, community outreach, school seminars, and interactive discussions to ensure that civic knowledge reaches even the most vulnerable populations.

What We Do

  • Conduct workshops and seminars on civic rights and responsibilities
  • Engage communities in discussions about leadership and governance
  • Raise awareness on legal protections and public service programs
  • Empower youth and women to participate actively in decision-making processes
  • Collaborate with local authorities to ensure information reaches marginalized areas
