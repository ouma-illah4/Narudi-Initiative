Our Programs

At Narudi Initiative, we focus on programs that uplift the most vulnerable members of our community.

Support Orphans

Providing shelter, education, and care for orphaned children.

We provide shelter, mentorship, school fees, and emotional support to orphaned children, ensuring they have the tools to grow and thrive in society.

Healthcare Assistance

Medical support and health services for the sick and needy.

Providing free medical checkups, vaccinations, health education, and emergency assistance to those in need.

Learn More

Support the Needy

Food, clothing, and essentials for families in need.

Emergency food, clothing, and basic necessities are provided to vulnerable families, helping them regain stability and dignity.

Addiction Recovery

Programs to help individuals overcome addiction and rebuild their lives.

Counseling, rehabilitation, and mentorship programs guide individuals struggling with addiction toward a healthy and purposeful life.

Civic Education

Raising awareness about human rights and active participation.

We educate communities on their rights, civic duties, and public participation to strengthen governance and social justice.

Climate & Environment

Building resilience against climate change impacts.

Programs focus on sustainable practices, tree planting, waste management, and climate change adaptation for communities.

Financial Literacy

Helping communities manage resources effectively.

Workshops and training sessions teach budgeting, saving, investments, and entrepreneurship to improve livelihoods.

Learn More

Mental Health Awareness

Raising awareness on mental health challenges in schools and communities.

We educate about depression, anxiety, abuse, and emotional well-being, offering counseling support and awareness programs in schools and community centers.
