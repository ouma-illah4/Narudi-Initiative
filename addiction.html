Addiction Recovery

Supporting youth to break the cycle and regain hope.

The Challenge in Kenya

Addiction among youth in Kenya, particularly in urban areas like Nairobi, is rising. This generation faces immense pressures: the influence of social media, internet culture, and the desire for improved living standards. Many turn to substances as a coping mechanism for stress, anxiety, and social pressures.

Coupled with growing unemployment, poverty, youth violence, and family instability, substance use becomes an escape for those struggling to navigate these challenges.

Key Facts

Over 10% of Kenyan youth report using addictive substances (UNODC 2022)
Rising alcohol and drug abuse linked to unemployment & poverty
Urban youth are most affected in informal settlements
Substance abuse increases risk of violence and mental health disorders

The Human Impact

Addiction affects every aspect of a young person’s life—relationships, education, career prospects, and mental health. Many youths experience isolation, depression, and an increased risk of criminal activity as they struggle with substance dependency.

Families and communities also feel the ripple effects, including increased healthcare burdens and social instability.

How We Help

At Narudi Initiative, we work to break the cycle by linking those affected with the appropriate support services:

  • Referrals to rehabilitation centers and professional counselors
  • Educational talks on substance abuse prevention
  • Counseling and mentorship programs for early intervention
  • In extreme cases, medical treatment and supervised recovery programs

Our approach combines awareness, emotional support, and professional help to ensure that affected youth have a pathway to recovery and a brighter future.
